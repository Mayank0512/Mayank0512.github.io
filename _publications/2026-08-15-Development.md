title: "Alizarin Red perturbs skeletal patterning and biomineralization via catalase inhibition"
collection: "publications"
category: "manuscripts"
permalink: "/publication/2026-08-15-Development"
excerpt: >-
  Alizarin Red is widely used as a vital stain for calcified structures in developing embryos.
  Here we show that it is not inert: in the sea urchin embryo, Alizarin Red perturbs skeletal
  patterning and biomineralization, and we identify catalase inhibition as the mechanism
  underlying these effects. These findings have implications for the interpretation of studies
  that rely on Alizarin Red as a passive label of skeletal development.
